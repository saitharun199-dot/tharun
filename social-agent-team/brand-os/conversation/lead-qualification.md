# Lead qualification
| Score | Signs | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 Hot | asks price/availability, has date, ready to buy | send booking link + notify owner now |
| 🌤 Warm | interested, comparing, no timeline | answer FAQs, follow up in 2 days |
| ❄️ Cold | just browsing, off-topic | polite reply, add to newsletter |
## Questions to ask (one at a time)
1. What are you looking for?  2. When do you need it?  3. Best way to reach you?
