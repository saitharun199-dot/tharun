# Crisis protocol
**Starts when:** 3+ negative reviews/mentions in 48h, a viral complaint, safety/legal issue, or media contact.
1. Orchestrator pauses all scheduled posts (set KILL_SWITCH to ON if serious).
2. Notify owner immediately with facts only.
3. Draft one holding statement: "We're aware of ___ and are looking into it. We'll update by ___."
4. No other public replies until the owner approves.
5. After it's resolved: write what happened + lessons here.
