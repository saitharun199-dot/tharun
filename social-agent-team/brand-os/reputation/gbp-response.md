# Google review response rules
## 5★ / 4★ (auto-approve)
"Thank you, [Name]! We're so glad [specific detail]. See you again soon. — [Owner name]"
## 3★ (owner approves)
Thank → acknowledge the specific point → what we're improving → invite back.
## 1★ / 2★ (owner approves — always)
"Hi [Name], I'm sorry your experience with [issue] wasn't what you expected. That's not the standard we aim for. Please contact me at [contact] so I can put this right. — [Owner name]"
## Never
Argue · blame · share booking details · offer refunds publicly · copy-paste the same reply twice in a row
