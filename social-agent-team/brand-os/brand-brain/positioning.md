# Positioning — Ride With Tharun
- **Name:** Ride With Tharun
- **What it is:** A motorcycle riding and travel channel on YouTube, with social media alongside
- **Who it's for:** Riders and travel lovers who want real road trips, routes and tips — and people dreaming of their first long ride
- **Where:** YouTube (main), Instagram, plus Shorts/Reels. Home base: Mancherial, Telangana
- **The one thing we give people:** The feeling of being on the ride, plus honest, useful info to plan their own
- **Why us, not other channels (3 reasons):**
  1. Real, unfiltered rides — friendly and fun, never fake hype
  2. Practical: routes, costs, stops and tips people can actually use
  3. Builds a crew — talks to viewers like friends
- **Bike on the channel:** Yamaha MT-15
- **Where we are now:** 47 YouTube subscribers (Nov 2026) — early stage, so the goal is growth and finding the crew
- **Best video so far:** https://youtu.be/pgYw9L3BoHY — use it as the model for hooks, length and style
- **Signature route:** the Maharashtra ride towards Lonavala (Western Ghats)
- **Main call to action:** "Subscribe and ride with us" / "Watch the full ride on YouTube"
- **Money:** none yet — focus is subscribers and watch time. YouTube ad revenue needs 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours (or 10M Shorts views); sponsorships later
