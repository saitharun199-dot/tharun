# Positioning — Ride With Tharun
- **Name:** Ride With Tharun
- **What it is:** A **Telugu travel vlog** on YouTube and Instagram — trips with the buddies (Dubai series, Somasila camping) plus rides on the MT-15 (Lonavala)
- **Handles:** YouTube @ridewiththarun1724 · Instagram @ridewiththarun1724
- **Who it's for:** Telugu-speaking travellers and riders (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Telugu people abroad) who want real trips, food, places and tips in their own language
- **Where:** YouTube (main), Instagram, plus Shorts/Reels. Home base: Mancherial, Telangana
- **The one thing we give people:** The feeling of being on the ride, plus honest, useful info to plan their own
- **Why us, not other channels (3 reasons):**
  1. Telugu storytelling with the buddies — friendly and fun, never fake hype
  2. Practical: routes, costs, stops and tips people can actually use
  3. Builds a crew — talks to viewers like friends
- **Bike on the channel:** Yamaha MT-15
- **Where we are now:** 47 YouTube subscribers (Nov 2026) — early stage, so the goal is growth and finding the crew
- **Best video so far:** https://youtu.be/pgYw9L3BoHY — use it as the model for hooks, length and style
- **Signature route:** the Maharashtra ride towards Lonavala (Western Ghats)
- **Main call to action:** "Subscribe for the rest of the series" / "Tell us where we should go next 👇"
- **Production:** shot on DJI, original music, chapters in every description. Tharun also edits travel and cinematic videos, and cinematic style is a big strength
- **What the audience loves most (Instagram, Sep–Nov 2026):** the bike, local people's stories (natural farming), festivals (Ganesh)
- **Published videos to reuse for Shorts/Reels:**
  - DUBAI Day 1 & 2 — https://youtu.be/haaz0KPLg54 (biryani, Dubai Mall, Burj Khalifa fountain, desert night drive)
