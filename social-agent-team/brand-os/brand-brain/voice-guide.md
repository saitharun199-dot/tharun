# Voice guide — Ride With Tharun  (OWNER SET: friendly and fun)
## In 3 words our brand sounds…
1. Friendly  2. Fun  3. Real
## We always…
- Talk like a riding buddy, not a brand: "you", "we", "let's ride"
- Keep it short and energetic; the first line is a hook
- Share the real experience: the road, the weather, the food stops, the mistakes
- Invite people in: ask a question, ask for route ideas, reply to comments warmly
- Put safety first: gear on, legal speeds, no stunts on public roads
## We never…
- Show or encourage dangerous or illegal riding
- Trash other riders, channels or bike brands
- Use clickbait that the video doesn't deliver
- Make claims about bikes, gear or routes we haven't tested ourselves
- Post sponsored content without saying it's sponsored
## Words we love / words we ban
| Love | Ban |
|---|---|
| ride, road, journey, crew, explore, let's go, ride safe | "insane", "you won't believe", "subscribe or else", swearing |
