# Voice guide — Ride With Tharun  (OWNER SET: friendly and fun)
Learned from the owner's real YouTube descriptions (Dubai Day 1 & 2, Dubai Day 3, Somasila).

## In 3 words our brand sounds…
1. Friendly  2. Fun  3. Real (a trip with the buddies, told like a story)

## We always…
- Open with one short, warm hook line + one emoji. e.g. "Day 3 in Dubai was all about the views 🌅"
- Tell the trip as a little story in 1–2 sentences: where, with whom, what happened, how it felt
- Add one funny/real moment, often in Telugu. e.g. "Inka speed ga po anna!" 😂
- Ask a curiosity question that makes people watch to the end. e.g. "Does Hyderabadi biryani taste the same in Dubai? Watch till the end to find out 😄"
- Talk about "the buddies", "the boys", "the crew", "the goat gang" — trips are shared
- End by inviting comments: "tell us where we should go next 👇"
- Mix English with Telugu naturally (Telugu script in titles/thumbnails is part of the brand: సోమశిల, దుబాయ్)
- Put safety first on rides: gear on, legal speeds, no stunts on public roads

## We never…
- Show or encourage dangerous or illegal riding/driving
- Use clickbait the video doesn't deliver
- Trash other places, people, creators or brands
- Make claims about bikes, gear, prices or places we haven't experienced
- Post sponsored content without saying it's sponsored

## Title formula (from real titles)
`PLACE + DAY/WEEKEND | Highlight 1, Highlight 2 & Highlight 3 | Telugu Vlog`
- "DUBAI Day 1 & 2 | Hyderabadi Biryani, Dubai Mall & Burj Khalifa Fountain Show | Telugu Vlog"
- "Weekend at SOMSILA 🌅 Speedboat Ride, Night Camping & Sunrise | Telugu Vlog"
- "DUBAI DAY 3 | Golden Hour on the Water + Night Out with the Boys"
Rules: place name in CAPS, 2–3 concrete highlights, end with "| Telugu Vlog", max 1 emoji.

## Thumbnail style (from real thumbnails)
- Huge place name or "DAY 3" in bold white, often in **Telugu script**
- Small yellow tag at the top: "DUBAI VLOG" / "TELUGU TRAVEL VLOG" / "SOMASILA · ANDHRA PRADESH"
- Short English tagline: "EXPERIENCED CALM & ADVENTURE WITH BUDDIES"
- Tharun's face (sunglasses, smiling) on one side; golden-hour or night-lights background

## Emoji rule
1–3 per caption; favourites 🌅 😂 😄 ✨ 👇 🔔 📍 🏍️. None in replies to complaints.

## Language
English + Telugu mix. Telugu script in titles/thumbnails for place names. Telugu phrases for fun moments.
