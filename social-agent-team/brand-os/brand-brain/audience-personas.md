## Persona 1: The weekend rider
- 20–35, owns a bike, rides on weekends with friends
- Wants: new routes, ride ideas, gear tips, good food stops
- Worries: safety, bike breakdowns, cost of long trips
- Online: YouTube, Instagram Reels, riding WhatsApp groups
- Watches when: a video shows a route they could actually do
## Persona 2: The dreamer
- 18–28, saving for a first big bike or first long trip
- Wants: to feel the ride, beginner tips, "how much does it cost"
- Worries: not knowing where to start, family worrying about safety
- Online: YouTube Shorts, Instagram
- Watches when: the video is exciting and the hook promises a story
