# Playbook: A post goes viral

**Trigger:** A post reaches 3× its normal engagement within 24 hours.

**Departments involved:** publishing-social, leads-conversations, content-engine, orchestrator

## Steps
1. Publishing flags the post and its numbers.
2. Leads & conversations watches comments and DMs closely; reply fast with FAQ answers.
3. Content engine drafts 2 follow-up posts on the same topic within 48h.
4. Pin a comment with a link or next step.
5. Paid boost suggestion → queue/pending/ with cost (owner decides).

## Measure it
Followers and leads gained in the 7 days after.

## Log it
Append to `logs/activity-log.md`: `date | publishing-social | playbook:a-post-goes-viral | <files> | done`
