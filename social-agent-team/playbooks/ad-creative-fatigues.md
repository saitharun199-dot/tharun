# Playbook: Ad creative fatigues

**Trigger:** An ad's click-through rate drops 30%+ from its best week, or frequency > 4.

**Departments involved:** creative-studio, results-roi, orchestrator

## Steps
1. Results & ROI confirms the drop with numbers.
2. Creative studio makes 3 fresh variations: new hook, new image, same offer.
3. Do not change budget. Swapping creative only; budget changes → owner.
4. Variations → queue/pending/ (ads always need approval).
5. After 7 days, keep the winner and log the lesson in roi-attribution.md.

## Measure it
Click-through rate back to its average.

## Log it
Append to `logs/activity-log.md`: `date | creative-studio | playbook:ad-creative-fatigues | <files> | done`
