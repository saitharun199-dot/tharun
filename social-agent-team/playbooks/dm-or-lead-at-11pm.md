# Playbook: DM or lead at 11pm

**Trigger:** A message or enquiry arrives outside business hours.

**Departments involved:** leads-conversations

## Steps
1. Reply within 5 minutes using dm-reply-scripts.md (out-of-hours script).
2. Answer simple questions from faq-library.md only.
3. Score the lead with lead-qualification.md.
4. Hot lead → send booking link; add to CRM; flag for owner first thing in the morning.
5. Never promise prices, discounts or dates that aren't in the FAQ.

## Measure it
Leads replied to within 5 minutes (target 100%).

## Log it
Append to `logs/activity-log.md`: `date | leads-conversations | playbook:dm-or-lead-at-11pm | <files> | done`
