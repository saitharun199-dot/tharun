## How to post a YouTube Short (on your phone)
1. Cut the clip: vertical (tall) video, under 60 seconds.
2. Open the **YouTube** app → tap **+** → **Upload a video** → pick the clip.
3. Paste the **Title** from this pack (it already has #Shorts).
4. Tap **Add description** → paste the **Description + hashtags**.
5. Audience: **"No, it's not made for kids"**. Visibility: **Public**.
6. Tap **Upload**. Copy the link and send it to us.

## How to post an Instagram Reel (on your phone)
1. Open **Instagram** → tap **+** → **Reel** → pick the same tall clip.
2. Add on-screen text if the pack says so → **Next**.
3. **Edit cover** → pick a frame, or add the cover image from the Canva folder.
4. Paste the **Caption + hashtags** from this pack.
5. Turn on **"Also share to Facebook"** only if you want to (optional).
6. Tap **Share**. Copy the link and send it to us.

*(Instagram carousel = tap **+** → **Post** → select up to 10 photos in order → paste caption → Share.)*
