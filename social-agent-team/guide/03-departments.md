# 🏢 THE 8 DEPARTMENTS
Each is a file in `.claude/agents/`. The orchestrator hands them work; you can also call one directly, e.g. *"Use the content-engine agent to write 3 Instagram posts about our new menu."*

| Department | What it does | Main tools (when connected) | Brand OS files |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Search visibility** | Google rankings, keywords, Google Business Profile posts | Search Console, Semrush, GA4, Ahrefs | signals-seo/ |
| **Market radar** | Watches competitors, trends and mentions | Semrush, Brand24, SparkToro, Similarweb, Alerts | competitor-watchlist |
| **Content engine** | Writes blogs, captions, newsletters | Claude, Surfer, Frase, Grammarly | content/, voice-guide |
| **Creative studio** | Image prompts, carousels, videos, voice-overs, ads | Canva, Midjourney, Remotion, Figma, ElevenLabs | creative/ |
| **Publishing & social** | Schedules and posts approved items | Metricool, WordPress, GBP, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok | social/ |
| **Reviews & reputation** | Replies to reviews, watches for crises | GBP, Podium, NiceJob | reputation/ |
| **Leads & conversations** | Answers DMs, scores leads, books calls | ManyChat, HubSpot, Twilio, Calendly, Front | conversation/ |
| **Results & ROI** | Weekly report, what earned money | Looker Studio, BigQuery, Metabase | reporting/ |

No tools connected yet? Every department still works from the Brand OS files and anything you paste in (for example, a screenshot of your Instagram insights).
